Basınç Dağılımı overlay işlevselliği nasıl kullanılır

Bu makale, Swing Catalyst & Motion Catalyst’in 10.1 versiyonuyla tanıtılan kuvvet vektörleri özelliğinin bir uzantısı olan basınç dağılımı video overlay işlevini göstermeyi amaçlamaktadır.

  • Uygulanabilir yazılım varyantları: Motion Catalyst & Swing Catalyst.

  • Versiyonlar: 10.2

  • Gerekli donanım: Denge Platformu veya Motion Plate (veya çift Motion Plate).

Which version of the software am I using?

For more details on force vectors please see our force vectors article.

Enabling the Pressure Distribution Bar’s

Just like force vectors, the pressure distribution bars require calibration.

Please note that there is a difference between performing a calibration in capture mode and in playback mode. If performed in capture it applies to all subsequent recordings. If performed in playback it applies only to that specific recording, therefore we recommend calibrating in capture mode so all new recordings automatically have the calibration set.

From the options it’s possible to change the transparency and the colors of the pressure distribution bars.

This is very similar to the existing functionality for force vectors.

Performing a new calibration

  • Make sure you are in capture mode if you wish for the calibration to be stored for new recordings.

  • Click “Data Visuals” & “Set markers!”.

  • With the mouse click on each corner of your Balance Plate or Motion Plate / Dual Motion Plate.

  • Save the calibration.

  • Click “Data Visuals” again, and turn “Pressure Distribution” to “ON”.
