Kuidas kasutada kaugtoetust

See artikkel kirjeldab, kuidas kasutada Swing Catalyst Remote Support rakendust, et võimaldada Swing Catalyst tehnilise toe meeskonnale ajutiselt sisse logida teie arvutisse ning juhtida hiirt ja klaviatuuri. Eeldatakse, et keegi Swing Catalyst toe meeskonnast andis teile juhise siia tulla.

Kaugjuhtimine on kasulik mitmel põhjusel:

  • Lahendada probleeme, mida on muul viisil raske e-posti ja telefoni teel kirjeldada

  • Kiirendada protsessi – e-postide vastuvõtmine ja saatmine võtab palju aega

  • Saada kohest abi, kui vajadus on kiire

Märkused

Swing Catalyst Remote Support on saadaval ainult klientidele, kellel on kehtiv Support & Update plaan. Põhitoetus hõlmab ainult e-posti suhtlust.

Meil ei ole juurdepääs teie arvutile ilma teie loata ja te saate igal ajal jälgida, mida tehakse. See rakendus on mõeldud ainult kliendi abistamiseks ja seda kasutatakse vastutustundlikult.

Samm 1 – avage Swing Catalyst

Peamenüüst minge Help jaotisesse ja klõpsake “Start Remote Support”.

A window will pop open with an ID number. Make sure to send that to your support representative when instructed to do so.

Alternative way to start remote support

  • Click the Windows start menu, if you have Windows 8, hit the Windows logo key on your keyboard.

  • Type in “remote support”. You should find Swing Catalyst Remote Support application. Click it to run.

  1.   The remote support application will show a dialog window similar to the picture below. Make note of the ID number below, 9 digits. Keep the application running to let support staff connect to your computer.

Step 3 - Notify Swing Catalyst Support Staff

Assuming you were instructed to follow this article by someone in the support staff, notify the support agent of the ID you just noted in the remote support application. Typically you will provide this ID through your support ticket. You do not need to give up any password, just the ID.

If you don’t have an open support ticket with us you can create a new one by contacting our support team. Explain what you need assistance with and include the ID to speed up the process.

A support agent will connect to your computer and communicate with you through the remote support application. When the session is over, you are free to close the Swing Catalyst remote support application. We will not be able to connect when it is not running.
