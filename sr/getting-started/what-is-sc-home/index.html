What is the Swing Catalyst Home?

The Home is a version of the Swing Catalyst software used aimed towards the home market. It has fewer professional features, such as lack of record lesson functionality and incorporates a user limit.

Home supports up to two live cameras and a Foresight or FlightScope launch monitor.

Differences between Home and Swing Catalyst Pro+ & Pro

Swing Catalyst Home targets the home user, and at a price point that is significantly lower than Swing Catalyst Pro, or Pro+ intended for professional users.
